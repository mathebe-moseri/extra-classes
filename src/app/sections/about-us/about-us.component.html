How To Apply

🎥 Not sure how to apply? Watch the short video below to see how to join our classes, or follow the 5 steps.

  1. 1

    Sign up. Tap Sign Up and fill in the learner's and parent/guardian's details.

  2. 2

    Check your email. We email you the enrolment contract. Print it and have the parent/guardian sign it.

  3. 3

    Pay the two weeks R150 fee using the banking details in "Payment Details" below, then reply to our email with 3 documents: proof of payment, your latest school report card, and the signed contract.

  4. 4

    Wait about an hour. We email you your login details.

  5. 5

    Log in and enjoy video lessons, exercises, and past question paper solutions explained.
